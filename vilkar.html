Har du en flyavgang, en eksamen eller en vakt du ikke kan miste, sett en alarm til.

Bildet vurderes av en kunstig intelligens, og den tar feil noen ganger. Den kan avvise riktig gjenstand, og den kan godkjenne noe den ikke burde. Blir bildet avvist, kan du ta et nytt.

What RiseUp is

RiseUp is an alarm clock with no off button. The alarm rings, you spin the reels and get an object, you find it and take a photo, and only then does the sound stop.

That is the product. You cannot snooze, and you cannot switch it off without completing the task. This is not a defect.

Read this before relying on it. RiseUp is not a medical device and is not suitable as your only alarm where oversleeping has serious consequences. If you have a flight, an exam or a shift you cannot miss, set a second alarm.

The image recognition

The photo is judged by an artificial intelligence, and it is sometimes wrong. It can reject the right object, and it can approve something it should not. If the photo is rejected, you can take another.

If the server does not answer at all, the photo is approved without judgement, so that you are not locked inside an alarm that cannot be stopped. That means the task can be bypassed by turning off the network. It is a deliberate trade-off: being able to stop the alarm always matters more than nobody being able to cheat.
