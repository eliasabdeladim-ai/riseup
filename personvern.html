Kort fortalt: RiseUp har ingen innlogging og spør deg aldri om navn, e-post eller telefonnummer. Bildet du tar om morgenen sendes til Google for å avgjøre om riktig gjenstand er på det, og slettes så snart svaret er gitt. Vi lagrer aldri bilder.

RiseUp er en vekkerklokke uten av-knapp. Når alarmen ringer, trekker appen en gjenstand du har hjemme, og alarmen stopper først når du har fotografert den.

Denne erklæringen forklarer hva appen samler inn, hvorfor, og hvem som får se det.

Hvem som er ansvarlig

Behandlingsansvarlige er Elias Abdeladim og Fredrik Moe, Norge, i fellesskap. Spørsmål om personvern, eller ønske om å få slettet dataene dine, sendes til riseup.supportmail@gmail.com.

Du har ingen konto

RiseUp har ingen registrering og ingen innlogging. Første gang du åpner appen, opprettes en anonym bruker som bare er et tilfeldig tall. Det tallet er ikke knyttet til deg, og vi kan ikke bruke det til å finne ut hvem du er.

Dette har en bakside du bør kjenne til: sletter du appen, forsvinner nøkkelen til den anonyme brukeren, og en ny installasjon starter på nytt. Streaken og historikken din blir da liggende igjen uten at noen, heller ikke vi, kan nå dem.

Bildet du tar

For å stoppe alarmen må du fotografere en gjenstand. Bildet sendes kryptert til vår server, som sender det videre til Google Gemini for å avgjøre om gjenstanden er på bildet. Svaret er fire opplysninger: om det ble godkjent, hva som ble gjenkjent, hvor sikker vurderingen er, og en kort begrunnelse.

Bildet lagres ingen steder. Det ligger i minnet mens vurderingen pågår, og forsvinner når svaret er sendt tilbake til telefonen. Det skrives ikke til noen database og ikke til noen fil. Google behandler bildet etter sine egne vilkår for utviklere, og bruker det ikke til å trene modellene sine.

Kameraet brukes bare i dette ene øyeblikket. Appen har ingen tilgang til bildegalleriet ditt.

In short: RiseUp has no sign-in and never asks for your name, email or phone number. The photo you take in the morning is sent to Google to decide whether the right object is in it, and is discarded as soon as the answer comes back. We never store photos.

RiseUp is an alarm clock with no off button. When the alarm rings, the app picks an object you have at home, and the alarm only stops once you have photographed it.

This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who gets to see it.

Who is responsible

The joint data controllers are Elias Abdeladim and Fredrik Moe, Norway. For privacy questions, or to have your data deleted, write to riseup.supportmail@gmail.com.

You do not have an account

RiseUp has no registration and no sign-in. The first time you open the app, an anonymous user is created that is nothing more than a random number. That number is not tied to you, and we cannot use it to work out who you are.

There is a downside you should know about: if you delete the app, the key to that anonymous user is gone, and a fresh install starts over. Your streak and history stay behind, and nobody, including us, can reach them.

The photo you take

To stop the alarm you have to photograph an object. The photo is sent encrypted to our server, which forwards it to Google Gemini to decide whether the object is in the picture. The answer is four pieces of information: whether it was approved, what was recognised, how confident the judgement is, and a short reason.

The photo is not stored anywhere. It exists in memory while the judgement is running and is gone once the answer is sent back to your phone. It is not written to any database and not to any file. Google processes the photo under its own developer terms and does not use it to train its models.

The camera is used only in that one moment. The app has no access to your photo library.

Who else is involved

We do not sell data and we do not share it with advertisers. The app carries no ads. These companies process data on our behalf, each for one specific job:

  • Supabase stores the anonymous user, the onboarding answers and the mornings. Servers in the EU.
  • Google (Gemini) judges the photo. The photo is not stored.
  • Amplitude counts events so we can see where people drop off.
  • Adapty keeps track of the subscription together with Apple.
  • Apple handles the payment itself. We never see your card.

Some of these are outside the EEA. Transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long

The onboarding answers and the mornings stay for as long as the anonymous user exists. If you ask for deletion, everything is removed within 30 days. Events in Amplitude are deleted automatically after 24 months.

Your rights

You can ask for access to what is stored, for correction, deletion, or a copy in a machine-readable format. Write to riseup.supportmail@gmail.com.

Because the account is anonymous, we cannot find your data from your email address alone. You will find your own user id in the app under Settings, and it has to be included in your request. Without it we do not know which rows are yours, and we will not guess.

If you believe we handle data wrongly, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

Children

RiseUp is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data about them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. If the change is significant, the app will tell you.
